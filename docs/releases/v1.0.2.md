DockPin 1.0.2: displays come back the way you arranged them after a restart, and dragged windows cross every border smoothly.

![The Dock on the left edge of the center monitor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/iosifnicolae2/DockPin/main/docs/dockpin-screens.png)

**What's fixed**
- After a restart, logout, crash or quit, your displays come back in your own arrangement. DockPin used to leave its pinned layout behind, and macOS then kept it as yours; it now puts your arrangement back whenever it stops, and never mistakes its own layout for yours.
- The pointer no longer jumps back or slows down after a restart.
- Dragging a window by its title bar crosses every display border smoothly: the window stays under the pointer at full speed, with no snap-backs.
- Fixed a rare crash with displays lined up side by side that share no edge.

**Install**
1. Download `DockPin.zip`, unzip it, and move `DockPin.app` to Applications (replace the old one).
2. Open it. Its icon appears in the menu bar.
3. Allow Accessibility when macOS asks (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility).

Requires macOS 14 or later. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
[How it works](https://github.com/iosifnicolae2/DockPin/blob/main/docs/how-it-works.md) explains the approach and its trade-offs.

The first release of DockPin: a menu-bar app that keeps a left-side macOS Dock on the center display of a row of monitors.

**What it does**
- Pins a left Dock to the display that has monitors on both sides, and keeps it there after display changes, sleep and login.
- Moving the pointer between the center display and its left neighbour feels like the real arrangement.
- Pushing the pointer at the other displays' left edges no longer pulls the Dock away.
- Opens at login (turn it off from the menu-bar icon). Quitting puts your display arrangement back.

**Install**
1. Download `DockPin-1.0.0.zip`, unzip it, and move `DockPin.app` to Applications.
2. Open it. Its icon appears in the menu bar; no permissions are needed.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
See the README for how it works and its trade-offs.

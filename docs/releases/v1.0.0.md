The first release of DockPin: a menu-bar app that keeps the macOS Dock on the center display of your monitors.

![The Dock on the left edge of the center monitor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/iosifnicolae2/DockPin/main/docs/dockpin-screens.png)

**What it does**
- Pins the Dock to the display that has monitors on both sides, with the Dock on the left, bottom or right, and keeps it there after display changes, sleep, login and Dock position changes.
- Moving the pointer across the center display's borders lands exactly where your real arrangement leads.
- Pushing the pointer at the other displays' Dock edges no longer pulls the Dock away.
- Pick the monitor (center by default) and the Dock position from the menu-bar icon.
- Opens at login (turn it off from the menu-bar icon). Quitting puts your display arrangement back.

**Install**
1. Download `DockPin-1.0.0.zip`, unzip it, and move `DockPin.app` to Applications.
2. Open it. Its icon appears in the menu bar.
3. Allow Accessibility when macOS asks (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility). It makes border crossings seamless; DockPin only watches pointer movement. Without it DockPin still works, with a brief pause at the moved border.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
[How it works](https://github.com/iosifnicolae2/DockPin/blob/main/docs/how-it-works.md) explains the approach and its trade-offs.
